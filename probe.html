where does a fixed layer actually end?

Start here. Paint the screen solid red. If the strip at the very bottom does not turn red, no page CSS can reach it and the water was never the problem.

If it does turn red, some rule reaches the bottom and the question is which. Each lettered column is a fixed box sized differently, painting its own bottom slice with a white line on its bottom edge. Show them and screenshot: a line on the physical bottom edge is a pass, a line above it is short, and no line at all means that box runs past the bottom — also a pass. The numbers are weaker evidence, being measured in a layout viewport that may itself be the thing falling short.
